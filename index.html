I am a PhD student in the Department of Government at Harvard University. My interests are broadly in the political economy of development, with a focus on public service delivery in sub-Saharan Africa. I am particularly interested in how bureaucrat selection, the built environment, and technology adoption shape the quality of these services. I say more on the research agenda page.

Before starting my PhD, I spent two years as a predoctoral research fellow with the Research Program in Development Economics at Princeton University.

I grew up in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, where I have also spent time working in government and the nonprofit sector. Outside of work, I love reading, especially contemporary American poetry.
